The Foundation Cellar

Laetentur Caeli

Let the heavens rejoice

Wines named for the people and moments that tried to make the Church breathe with both lungs

Why Wine

Two Places, One Table

Laetentur caeli, “let the heavens rejoice,” are the first words of the decree of union read at the Council of Florence in 1439, when Greek and Latin bishops stood together and declared the Church one. The union did not last. The hope did.

The Likoudis family came from Kefalonia and settled in the Finger Lakes country of upstate New York. The wines come from those two places. Each label tells one story of East and West, and every bottle supports the Foundation’s research, the Fellows Program, and The Kydones Review.

First Release · Finger Lakes

The Brothers of Thessaloniki

Saints Cyril and Methodius, born in Thessaloniki, carried the Gospel to the Slavs in their own language and were honored in Rome and Constantinople alike. Their wines are sold together, a white and a red in one box.

White

St. Cyril

A dry Finger Lakes Riesling. Bright, mineral, and clear, for the brother who gave the Slavs an alphabet and died in Rome in 869.

Red

St. Methodius

A Finger Lakes Cabernet Franc. Firm and patient, for the brother who carried the mission on for sixteen more years as archbishop.

Second Release · Kefalonia

From the Family’s Island

Three wines from the island’s own grapes: Robola, its signature white, and Mavrodaphne, which Kefalonian growers make into a dry red and a dry rosé.

White · Robola

Kydones

For Demetrios Kydones of Thessaloniki, who translated Thomas Aquinas into Greek so East and West could read each other.

Red · Mavrodaphne

Florence 1439

For the Council of Florence, where Greek and Latin bishops signed one decree of union and read it aloud in both tongues.

Rosé · Mavrodaphne

St. Leopold Mandić

For the Capuchin confessor from the Adriatic coast who offered his whole life for the reunion of the Eastern Churches with Rome.

The Likoudis Legacy Foundation does not make, sell, or ship wine. A licensed Finger Lakes winery makes, bottles, sells, and ships the first release under the Foundation’s labels, and the Kefalonian wines will come through a licensed importer and its retail partners. For every bottle sold, the winery sends a set amount to the Foundation. Purchases are not tax-deductible donations. You must be 21 or older to buy wine.

A 501(c)(3) research and educational foundation dedicated to ecumenical scholarship, Christian unity, and the promotion of religious liberty worldwide.
