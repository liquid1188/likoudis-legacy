The October Pilgrimage · 2027

Greece, East and West

Between two feasts, St. Gerasimos on Kefalonia and St. Demetrios in Thessaloniki, in the footsteps of St. Paul

October 16 to 27, 202712 daysAbout $3,495 with air
An Annual Pilgrimage

Two Feasts, Six Days Apart

Every October the Church in Greece keeps two great feasts. On October 20, Kefalonia carries the relics of St. Gerasimos in procession through the Omala valley. On October 26, Thessaloniki keeps the feast of its patron St. Demetrios, a saint honored by Catholics and Orthodox alike, on the same day the city marks its liberation in 1912.

The Foundation’s pilgrimage travels between them, from the island of the Likoudis family to the city of Demetrios Kydones. We plan to make it a yearly journey, held each October between the two feasts.

Why Greece

Where the Two Lungs Meet

James Likoudis spent his life on the question of Catholic and Orthodox unity. This pilgrimage walks the ground where that question began. St. Paul carried the Gospel into Europe at Philippi, wrote to the churches of Thessalonica and Corinth, and preached before the Areopagus in Athens.

Thessaloniki is also the city of Demetrios Kydones, the fourteenth-century Byzantine statesman and translator whose name the Foundation’s journal carries. We visit his city, the basilica of its patron St. Demetrios, and both Catholic and Orthodox communities in Greece today.

Along the way we go home to Kefalonia, the island the Likoudis family came from, and cross to Ithaka, where the family name still stands above the harbor at Kioni.

The Pilgrimage

Prayer, Study, and Place

This is a pilgrimage with a curriculum. Each day pairs a holy site with a short talk and a reading, so pilgrims come home understanding what they saw.

  • Daily Mass with a priest chaplain
  • An Orthodox Divine Liturgy, with an introduction beforehand
  • Short daily talks on St. Paul, the councils, and the schism
  • A printed reading guide prepared by the Foundation
  • Licensed local guides at every major site
  • Hotels, ground transport, and most meals
Itinerary

Twelve Days

A working outline for 2027. Final times and hotels are set with our tour operator.

  1. 1
    Sat Oct 16

    Depart the United States

    Overnight flight to Athens.

  2. 2
    Sun Oct 17

    Athens

    Arrival and check in. Evening Mass and welcome dinner.

  3. 3
    Mon Oct 18

    The Areopagus

    The Acropolis and the Areopagus, where St. Paul spoke of the unknown god. Mass at the Catholic Cathedral of St. Dionysius the Areopagite. Reading from Acts 17.

  4. 4
    Tue Oct 19

    Corinth, Then the Sea

    Ancient Corinth and the Bema where St. Paul stood before Gallio. West across the Peloponnese to Kyllini and by ferry to Kefalonia. Reading from 1 Corinthians 12 and 13.

  5. 5
    Wed Oct 20

    The Feast of St. Gerasimos

    The procession of the relics at the Monastery of St. Gerasimos in the Omala valley, with an introduction to the Divine Liturgy beforehand. Talk on James Likoudis and the Kefalonian roots of the family.

  6. 6
    Thu Oct 21

    Ithaka

    Ferry from Sami across the channel to Ithaka. Lunch in Kioni above the harbor.

  7. 7
    Fri Oct 22

    North to Meteora

    Ferry to the mainland and north by coach to Kalambaka beneath the rocks.

  8. 8
    Sat Oct 23

    Meteora

    The monasteries on the rocks, and a conversation on Eastern monastic life. Afternoon drive to Thessaloniki.

  9. 9
    Sun Oct 24

    Thessaloniki

    Sunday Mass. The Rotunda, Hagia Sophia, and the city walls. Reading from 1 Thessalonians.

  10. 10
    Mon Oct 25

    Philippi and Kavala

    Where St. Paul first preached in Europe and baptized Lydia. Mass near the river. Reading from Acts 16 and Philippians.

  11. 11
    Tue Oct 26

    The Feast of St. Demetrios

    The Basilica of St. Demetrios on its feast day. Talk on Demetrios Kydones and the Byzantine Thomists. Farewell dinner.

  12. 12
    Wed Oct 27

    Home

    Return flight from Thessaloniki.

Before You Go

The Reading Guide

Every pilgrim receives a short printed guide with the day’s Scripture, a page of history for each site, and selections from the Foundation’s own work.

  • The Acts of the Apostles, chapters 16 to 18
  • St. Paul’s letters to the Thessalonians, Philippians, and Corinthians
  • Selections from James Likoudis, Ending the Byzantine Greek Schism
  • An essay from The Kydones Review
Details

Dates and Price

October 16 to 27, 2027. Our working estimate is about $3,495 per person with round trip air from Washington, or about $2,395 for pilgrims who arrange their own flights. Prices assume a shared room and a small group of about fifteen, and will be confirmed when registration opens.

We keep the cost low on purpose. October is past the summer season, the group stays in comfortable, central three and four star hotels, and pilgrims fly into Athens and home from Thessaloniki without doubling back.

  • Ten nights in hotels with breakfast daily
  • Eight dinners, including welcome and farewell
  • Private coach, ferries, licensed guides, and site entry
  • A $500 deposit holds your place once registration opens

Arrangements are made through an established, registered Catholic pilgrimage operator. Add your name below to hear first. There is no commitment.
