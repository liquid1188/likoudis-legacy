Short Reviews Of "HERALDS OF A CATHOLIC RUSSIA"
A book by Dr. James Likoudis

 

 

The Reverend Ray Ryland, Ph.D., J.D.
Author of "Drawn From The Shadows Into Thruth
"James Likoudis is a noted Catholic writer on the separated Eastern Churches. There is much to be learned from his stirring accounts of Easterners who embraced their true home, the Catholic Church."

 


Dennis Bonnette, Ph.D.
Professor of Philosophy (Retired, Niagara University)
"The witness of Russian and Greek Orthodox reconciled to the Catholic Church is remarkable testimony for the need of a Papal primacy of universal authority in the Church for the unity of truth, the unity of Revelation, and the visible unity of the Church as taught in Vatican I and II."

 


Fr. Edouard Perrone
Pastor, Assumption Grotto, Detroit, MI
"These collected accounts of twelve spiritual pilgrims seeking truth cry out with particular urgency for the end of the tragic Byzantine Greco-Slav Schism. They give new reason that Our Lady of Fatima prophesied a future Catholic Russia."

James Likoudis is an expert in Catholic apologetics. He has written many articles published by various religious papers and magazines.
