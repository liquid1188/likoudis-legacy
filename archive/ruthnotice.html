NOTICE OF DEATH of RUTH LIKOUDIS
Beloved wife of Dr. James Likoudis


Sadly we announce the passing of Ruth, faithful wife of Dr. James Likoudis for over seven decades. She is remembered as a loving and caring wife, mother of six and grandmother of 29. As a Catholic, her Faith was exemplary, her Love and Charity genuine, and her Hope was in the Lord. On April 14, 2023 with her family around her, she passed away and presented herself to her Lord, and may she hear those sweet and consoling words "WELL DONE, GOOD AND FAITHFUL SERVANT, ENTER THE KINGDOM OF MY FATHER".
Ruth, may the Lord give you rest and shine His eternal Light on you.

In loving memory of my cherished wife, Ruth, who walked by my side as my steadfast companion throughout our remarkable 71-year marriage. Together, we built a beautiful life and were blessed to create a flourishing family, welcoming six wonderful children, 35 loving grandchildren, and 44 precious great-grandchildren into the world. As a devout Catholic, Ruth's life was a shining example of piety, humility, and unwavering obedience to God's will. Her faith, hope, and love served as a guiding light for our family, leaving an enduring legacy that will continue to uplift and inspire us. With a heart weighed down by sorrow yet filled with hope, I entrust her soul to the divine mercy of our Lord and the intercession of the Blessed Virgin Mary.
