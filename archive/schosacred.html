Dr. Likoudis shows the Dominicans' Efforts at Reconciliation with the Orthodox

 

This superb in-depth study of the Catholic–Orthodox reunion efforts, during the tumultuous 11th–15th centuries, reveals the vital role played by the Dominican Order in shaping the theological and political discourse of that era, with a focus on the contributions of prominent Dominican figures such as John Stojkovich of Ragusa, Andrew Chrysoberges, Manuel Calecas, Demetrios Cydones, Thomas Aquinas and many others who played a critical role in the events leading to the Council of Florence.
