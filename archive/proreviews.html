"His Broken Body: Understanding and Healing the Schism between the Roman Catholic and Eastern Orthodox Churches"
By Fr. Laurent A. Cleenewerck
Euclid University Consortium Press - 446 pages
Dr. Likoudis' lengthy analysis (20 xeroxed pages) of Eastern Orthodox priest Fr. Laurent A. Cleenewerck's book "His Broken Body: Understanding and Healing the Schism between the Roman Catholic and Eastern Orthodox Churches" (446 pages: Euclid University Consortium Press) is now available. In this review Dr. Likoudis addresses the mistreatment of the historical and theological evidence for the Petrine Primacy in the first centuries of the Church.

Dr. James Likoudis' incivise and penetrating perspective cuts through the tangle of problematic objections and issues by the Eastern Orthodox as these relate to the Catholic Church. In this book review, Dr. Likoudis is able to demonstrate that consequent to the Schism from the "Cathedra Petri" the Orthodox lost their Catholicity, and therefore no longer possess Catholic Unicity. Thus, the Orthodox, rationalizing this lack of Unity with The Apostolic See (also among themselves), has resulted in a deeply flawed Theology, which in turn begets a faulty Ecclesiology (remember: Lex Orandi, Lex Credendi). In 20 pages or so, Dr. Likoudis responds point by point, to the how's and the why's of Eastern Orthodox' objections in the book "This Broken Body..." by Fr. Laurent Cleenewerck.
